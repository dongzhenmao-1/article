### end up
- **end up with**: ultimately get sth. or be faced with sth.  
  She argued with boss and ended up with no job.
- **end up in**: ultimately reach some place or state.  
  If you keep driving like that, you will end up in the hospital.
- **end up as**: ultimately become.  
    He started as an intern and ended up as the manager of the department.
- **end up doing**: ultimately do.
- **end up + adj.**: ultimately.
